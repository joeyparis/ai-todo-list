## Design Context

### Users
Casual/experimental users trying out AI-assisted task management. Not mission-critical - they're exploring what AI can do for personal productivity. Context is personal devices, varied environments.

### Brand Personality
Calm, focused, confident. The interface should feel like a well-made tool that gets out of your way. Not precious, not playful - just smooth and intentional. Think Things 3, Linear.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Quiet confidence. Motion should feel inevitable, like water settling.
- **Theme**: Light/dark with class-based toggle. Surface palette is slate-tinted.
- **Typography**: Inter (system-like, neutral).
- **Color**: Indigo primary, slate surface. Restrained use of accent color.
- **Anti-references**: Duolingo (too playful), Notion (too busy), generic AI dashboards (neon-on-dark).
- **References**: Things 3 (animation quality), Linear (information density + polish), Apple Reminders (gesture feel).

### Design Principles
1. **Motion serves comprehension** - Every animation explains a spatial relationship or state change. No decoration.
2. **Calm confidence** - High-damping springs, smooth deceleration. Never bouncy, never elastic.
3. **Invisible when right** - The best animation is one users don't consciously notice but would miss if removed.
4. **Respect the user** - Always honor prefers-reduced-motion. Never block interaction during animation.
5. **Progressive sophistication** - Simple on the surface, technically precise underneath.
